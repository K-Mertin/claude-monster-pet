# Changelog

## 0.7.1
- Feeding, playing, talking and other care now wake a tucked-in monster (a little grumpy) and count as you being around.
- A Wake up button and `/pet wake` end a tuck-in early.
- The status says how long a tucked-in monster will sleep.
- A failed `/pet` command now shows a plain error instead of debug output.

## 0.7.0
- Friends' visits: `/pet visit <DNA code>` brings a friend's monster to stay for a day, drawn from their code and strolling beside yours. Play together or spar once per visit (`/pet visit play|spar|bye`). `/pet visit` shows your own code.
- Two new badges: Good host and Sparring partner.

## 0.6.0
- First-run tour: one tip at a time above the prompt, moving on as you try each thing (`/pet tour`, `/pet tour skip`).
- "Next up" in the habitat: the single most useful thing to do now.
- Seven starter quests with small rewards.
- `/pet help` lists every command by group.
- Fixed: the Style tab failed to draw once a hat was unlocked.
- Tests now draw the band and the habitat on the terminal and the desktop.

## 0.5.0
- Habit DNA: your languages, working hours, commit size, tests and a seed from your git email shape your monster's colour, pattern, mark, build, armour, eyes and extras. One in 256 is shiny.
- `/pet card` saves a shareable pixel card with a DNA code.

## 0.4.0
- Weekly Bug Boss built from the previous week's failures, fought with Strike, Outsmart and Dodge.
- Secret forms (Archivist, Bugslayer, Goldheart) and generations with a Hall of Fame.
- Seasons, daily weather and holidays in the habitat; a gem shop with eight decorations; the monster strolls and naps in its bed.

## 0.3.0
- Play the treat hunt by typing (`/pet hunt left`), ♥ and 💬 buttons in the band, plain replies for every command, one-time alerts and optional 8-bit sounds.

## 0.2.0
- Items earned from work (cookies, coffee, gems, bug snacks), the treat hunt, training, hats, badges, talking, daily streaks, personalities, and bright or shadow adult forms.

## 0.1.0
- A monster that hatches and grows from your Claude Code work, with four evolution lines and a habitat.
